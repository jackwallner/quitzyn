## Quit Zyn for Android Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Quit Zyn (listed on Google Play as Quit Zyn: Pouch & Snus Tracker, published by E3 Apps) is a personal tracker for quitting nicotine on Android. It does not require an account.

## Data stored on your device

Your quit date, check-ins, mood, notes, journal entries, craving sessions, garden state, achievements, spending and pouch estimates, settings, and reminder preferences are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. This information is not uploaded, and it is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

## Purchases

Bloom+ is offered through monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Google Play processes payment. RevenueCat verifies purchase status and may process an anonymous app user ID, purchase and entitlement information, simple counts of when the upgrade screen was shown (for example, how many times and on which screen), and limited technical context such as app version and device type. It never receives your journal, check-ins, notes, cravings, or other app content. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Notifications

If you allow reminders, the app schedules them on your phone. Their text is created on the device and nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

## What we do not collect

No account, advertising, ad identifiers, analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, location, contacts, photos, or health platform data. We do not sell or share personal information.

## Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your phone until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app, which removes it. Google and RevenueCat keep transaction records as needed to process and restore purchases. Support emails contain whatever you choose to send and are used only to answer you.

**To delete your data from Quit Zyn: Pouch & Snus Tracker (E3 Apps):**

1. Your quit records, journal, cravings and settings: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Quit Zyn > Storage > Clear storage. None of this is stored off your phone.
2. Data held by RevenueCat for your anonymous purchase ID (purchase history, app-open and purchase-screen counts): email [jackwallner+qz@gmail.com](mailto:jackwallner+qz@gmail.com?subject=Quit%20Zyn%20Android%20data%20deletion) with the subject "Quit Zyn Android data deletion". We delete it within 30 days. Google Play order records are kept by Google under its own policy and are not deleted by this request.

## Health information

Quit Zyn shows general wellness information with sources. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

## Children and changes

Quit Zyn is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We may update this policy when the app or its services change.
