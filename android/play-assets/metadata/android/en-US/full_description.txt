Quit Zyn makes going nicotine-free simple and calm. Count every day off the pouches, check in once a day, and watch a bonsai grow as your streak builds. Whether you're quitting Zyn, snus, nicotine pouches, dip, chewing tobacco or vaping, everything stays private on your phone. No account needed.

If you want one quiet place to see how far you've come, ride out cravings and keep your nicotine-free streak going, this is that app.

YOUR PROGRESS AT A GLANCE (FREE)
- A quit day counter, your longest streak and every nicotine-free day on a calendar.
- One-tap daily check-in, with an optional mood and note.
- A week strip that fills in as you show up.
- A daily reminder at the hour you choose.

A GARDEN THAT GROWS WITH YOU (FREE)
- A bonsai that grows a little with every nicotine-free day.
- New stages appear as your streak builds, and finished trees join your grove.
- A living reminder of how far you've come.

WHEN A CRAVING HITS (FREE)
- Craving mode: a two-minute guided breathing session to ride out an urge.
- Note what set it off (coffee, driving, a work break), if you want to.
- A slip doesn't erase your garden. Your counter restarts honestly, your tree keeps half its growth, and a mistaken slip can be undone.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN (FREE)
- A home screen widget with your day count and your tree.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN (FREE)
- Your journey, journal and cravings stay on your device.
- No account. No ads. No tracking.

BLOOM+ (OPTIONAL)
An upgrade unlocks the full experience:
- Your craving patterns: when urges hit, what sets them off and how long yours last
- The complete nicotine-recovery timeline with sources
- A journal with guided prompts
- Achievements and milestone unlocks
- Money, pouches and nicotine avoided since day one
- Five more bonsai species to grow
New subscribers can start with a free trial.

WHO IT'S FOR
Anyone quitting nicotine pouches, snus, dip or a vape, cutting back on smokeless tobacco, or getting through the first weeks of nicotine withdrawal and cravings. Set the day you quit (or start today) and the app keeps count of every nicotine-free day. With Bloom+, it also tallies the money kept and pouches skipped, based on how many you used.

Quit Zyn keeps it simple: count your nicotine-free time, check in, and watch your garden grow.
