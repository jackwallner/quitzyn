## Why is there no account?

Your quit date, check-ins, journal and cravings are stored on your iPhone and shared only with the app's own widgets and Watch app. None of it is uploaded, so there is nothing to sign in to. There are no ads and no cross-app tracking, and tracking works without an internet connection.

If you buy Bloom+, Apple takes the payment and RevenueCat checks the purchase using an anonymous ID. The app also attaches a few anonymous counts about the upgrade offer to that ID, such as how often it was shown. Neither ever receives your check-ins or journal. The [privacy policy](https://jackwallner.com/ios/quitzyn/privacy-policy.html) lists the details.

The trade-off of keeping everything on one phone: there is no sync between devices.

## Does it work for snus and other pouches?
