### What Mattered

**The garden.** Streak trackers usually reduce months of not using nicotine to a number. Giving that number a living, visible form (a bonsai that ages and changes species as your streak grows) made the app something people actually check daily instead of dreading.

**Slips don't erase your history.** Log a slip honestly and the calendar marks the day red. Everything before it stays.

**Recovery timeline backed by real sources.** Every health-benefit claim cites the ACS, NCI, or AHA. Nothing is invented or exaggerated, and the app never claims to treat, cure, or diagnose anything.

**Privacy.** No accounts, no ads, and nothing you write or log ever leaves your device. Purchases go through Apple and RevenueCat with an anonymous ID. The app has no idea who you are, which matters for something this personal.

## Questions

Quick answers about the quit Zyn app.

## Is there a free app to help me quit Zyn?

Yes. Quit Zyn: Pouch & Snus Tracker is free to download. It counts the days since your last pouch, gives you a daily check-in, a calendar, a growing bonsai garden and a craving mode, and the optional Bloom+ upgrade adds extras such as the full recovery timeline and journal.

## How do I track how long I have been off nicotine pouches?

Set your quit date and Quit Zyn counts your nicotine-free days and hours and keeps your longest streak. Check in once a day to log how you feel and water your garden.

## Does it work for snus, dip or vaping too?

Yes. The app is built around nicotine pouches and snus, and it works as a nicotine-free day counter for Zyn, dip or vaping too.

## What happens if I slip?

You log the slip yourself and the counter restarts honestly from that day. Your garden keeps half its growth, and a slip logged by mistake can be undone.

## What can I do when a craving hits?

Open craving mode for a short guided breathing session, and note what set the urge off if you want to. With Bloom+, the app also shows your craving patterns over time.

## Does it have widgets or an Apple Watch app?

Yes. Your day count shows in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and the Apple Watch companion shows your streak.

## Do I need an account, and is it private?

No account is needed. Quit Zyn has no ads or tracking, and your journey, journal and cravings stay on your device.

## Is Quit Zyn a medical or treatment app?

No. It is a tracking and motivation tool, it does not diagnose or treat nicotine dependence, and it is not a substitute for a doctor or a quit program. Talk to a healthcare professional about your own situation.
