How is the cost worked out?

Can price divided by pouches per can gives the price of one pouch. Multiply by pouches per day for a daily cost, then by 7, by 365 and by 365 divided by 12 for a week, a year and a month. At 10 pouches a day and $6 for a 15-pouch can, one pouch is $0.40, so the habit is $4 a day, $28 a week and $1,460 a year.

How much nicotine is that?

The first line is the label: pouches per day times the strength printed on the can. The Zyn sold in the United States comes in 3 mg and 6 mg.

The second line is what leaves the pouch while you use it. In a 2020 study in Nicotine & Tobacco Research, people drew a little over half of the label amount out of a Zyn pouch.

The calculator applies that 50 to 59 percent range to your number. One of the study's four authors worked for Swedish Match, which makes Zyn.

How does that compare with cigarettes?

A smoker absorbs about 1 to 1.5 mg of nicotine from one cigarette, according to a review by Benowitz, Hukkanen and Jacob. The third line divides the nicotine your pouches release by that range. Nicotine that leaves a pouch is not all absorbed, so read it as a rough upper comparison. It counts nicotine only and says nothing about health risk.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Zyn habit cost per year?

Divide the can price by the pouches in a can, then multiply by pouches per day and by 365. At 10 pouches a day and $6 for a 15-pouch can, that is $4 a day, $28 a week and $1,460 a year.

How much nicotine is in a Zyn pouch?

The label says 3 mg or 6 mg for the Zyn sold in the United States. In a 2020 study, people drew about 1.5 mg out of a 3 mg pouch and 3.5 mg out of a 6 mg pouch, so a little over half of the label amount left the pouch.

How many cigarettes is one Zyn equal to?

By nicotine alone, roughly 2 to 3.5 cigarettes for a 6 mg pouch and 1 to 2 for a 3 mg pouch. That compares the 50 to 59 percent of nicotine a pouch releases with the 1 to 1.5 mg a smoker absorbs from a cigarette. It is not a comparison of health risk.

How many Zyn pouches are in a can?

A can of Zyn sold in the United States holds 15 pouches. Change the number in the calculator if your brand or country packs a different count.

Does this calculator save what I type?

No. The math runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere or stored, and it is gone when you close the page.
