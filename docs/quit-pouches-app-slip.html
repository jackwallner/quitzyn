Is there a quit pouches app where a slip doesn't reset everything?

By Jack Wallner · Last updated October 30, 2026

Yes. In Quit Zyn: Pouch & Snus Tracker, a slip restarts the day counter and keeps the rest of your record. Your calendar history, longest streak and total nicotine-free days stay, the garden keeps half of its growth, and a slip logged by mistake can be undone. Slip logging is free.

Disclosure: Jack Wallner makes Quit Zyn, so this page describes his own app. Features and what is free were checked against the app as of October 2026.

General information, not medical advice. Quit Zyn is a tracking tool and does not treat nicotine dependence. For help quitting, talk to a clinician or call 1-800-QUIT-NOW (1-800-784-8669) in the US, and see Smokefree.gov.

What happens when you log a slip?

  • The day shows on your calendar in red, with an optional note on what was going on.
  • The current count restarts. A slip today makes today day 1. For a slip on a past date, the days since then are counted.
  • Your longest streak and total nicotine-free days stay, with the longest streak adjusted to the corrected dates.
  • The garden keeps half of the growth it had. A tree grown over 40 days goes back to about 20 days of growth and keeps growing from there.
  • Trees you have already completed stay in your garden.

Why does the counter still restart?

The counter shows how many days it has been since your last pouch. The longest streak and total show how far you have come overall. Quit Zyn puts all three side by side on the calendar, so a slip changes the first number and leaves the rest of your record in place.

What if I log a slip by mistake?

Tap Undo, I didn't slip. It reverses the slip in full, including the counter, the calendar day and the garden, as long as it is still your most recent slip. Logging the same day twice counts as one slip.

Can I log a slip from a craving?

Yes. If you end a craving breathing session with I gave in, the app offers the slip screen. Nothing changes until you confirm.

Is it free?

Yes. Slip logging, undo, the calendar, the garden and the day counter are free, along with craving mode, widgets and the Apple Watch app. See the full free and Bloom+ list.

What are the limits?

Quit Zyn is built for stopping completely. It has no per-pouch logging, no daily limit or taper plan and no nicotine strength tracking. If you want to cut down gradually first, the quit Zyn app comparison covers apps built for tapering. Quit Zyn has no App Store ratings yet.

Frequently asked questions

Does a slip delete my history in Quit Zyn?

No. Your calendar, longest streak, total nicotine-free days and completed trees stay. Only the current count restarts.

Can I log a slip on an earlier date?

Yes. Tap that day on the calendar to log it, and the days since then are counted.

How much of the garden does a slip keep?

Half of the growth the tree had when you slipped.

Can I undo a slip?

Yes, while it is still your most recent slip. Undo restores the counter, the calendar day and the garden.
