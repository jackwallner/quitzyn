Is there a Quit Zyn app for Android?

By Jack Wallner · Last updated October 30, 2026

Yes. Quit Zyn: Pouch & Snus Tracker is on Google Play for Android. It counts the days since your last Zyn, snus or other nicotine pouch, with a daily check-in, a calendar, a guided breathing session for cravings and a Home Screen widget. There is no account to create.

Disclosure: Jack Wallner makes Quit Zyn, so this page describes his own app. What the Android app does was checked against its code and its Google Play listing as of October 2026.

General information, not medical advice. Quit Zyn is a self-tracking tool and does not treat nicotine dependence. For help quitting, talk to a clinician or call 1-800-QUIT-NOW (1-800-784-8669) in the US, and see Smokefree.gov.
Quit Zyn is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the makers of ZYN or any other nicotine brand.

What does Quit Zyn do on Android?

  • Day counter: you set the day you quit, or start today, and the app counts every nicotine-free day, with your longest streak and a calendar.
  • Daily check-in: one tap, with an optional mood and note, and a week strip that fills in as you show up.
  • Reminder: a daily reminder at the hour you choose.
  • Bonsai garden: a tree that grows with your nicotine-free days, and finished trees join your grove.
  • Craving mode: a two-minute guided breathing session for an urge, with an optional note on what set it off, such as coffee, driving or a work break.
  • Slips: your counter restarts from that day, your tree keeps half its growth, and a slip you logged by mistake can be undone.

What does the Android widget show?

The Home Screen widget shows your days nicotine-free with your bonsai. Stretch it wider and it also shows your tree stage and the date you started. To add it, touch and hold an empty spot on your Home screen, open Widgets and choose Quit Zyn.

Frequently asked questions

Is Quit Zyn available on Android?

Yes. Quit Zyn: Pouch & Snus Tracker is on Google Play for Android 8.0 and newer, and it appears on your phone as Quit Zyn.

Is the Quit Zyn Android app free?

Yes. It is free to install, and the day counter, calendar, check-in, craving mode and Home Screen widget are free. Bloom+ is an optional upgrade.

Does Quit Zyn for Android have a widget?

Yes. A Home Screen widget shows your days nicotine-free with your bonsai, and a wider size adds your tree stage and start date. It can be resized.

Does Quit Zyn on Android work for snus and other pouches?

Yes. It counts nicotine-free days, so it works for Zyn, other pouch brands, snus, dip, chewing tobacco or vaping.

Do I need an account?

No. Quit Zyn needs no account, and your journey, journal and cravings stay on your phone.
