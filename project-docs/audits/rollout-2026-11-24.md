## Good moments for the star prompt

1. Daily check-in tapped, and the welcome-back "Still nicotine-free" tap: the core action of the app, done by the user a moment ago. It asks when the note sheet closes if that sheet opened, else after 3.5 s.
2. Garden growth celebration finished: the app's clearest positive result.
3. A craving ridden out ("It passed"): the highest-intent good result. Gave in and not-sure outcomes never ask, and neither does a slip.

Never on launch, onboarding, a slip, or around a paywall or trial offer.

## Notes on the control arm

The three surfaces never agreed on the trial button copy, so the control is per surface: onboarding "Start my N-day free trial" (sentence case), trial sheet "Start My N-Day Free Trial", paywall the selected plan's own intro title ("Start 7-Day Free Trial"). The `seven_day` arm drops the pronoun on onboarding and the sheet, and equals control on the paywall.
