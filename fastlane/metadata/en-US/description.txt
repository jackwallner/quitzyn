Count the days since you quit Zyn. Quit Zyn is a free day counter for nicotine pouches, snus, dip, vaping and cigarettes, with guided breathing for cravings, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and an Apple Watch app. No account and no sign-up. Your data stays on your iPhone.

COUNT DAYS SINCE YOUR LAST POUCH (FREE)
- Set your quit date, today or any day in the past, and see how many days you've been nicotine-free.
- Current streak, longest streak and total nicotine-free days on one calendar.
- One-tap daily check-in to log how the day felt.
- A daily reminder at the hour you choose.

CRAVING MODE WITH GUIDED BREATHING (FREE)
- Tap "I'm having a craving" for a box breathing session: breathe in, hold, breathe out, hold, four seconds each.
- Two minutes by default, with another minute whenever you need it.
- Note how strong the craving was and what set it off, if you want to.
- See how many cravings you've ridden out.

WHEN YOU SLIP (FREE)
- Log a slip, today or on an earlier date, and the day counter restarts from there.
- Your calendar history, longest streak and total nicotine-free days stay.
- Your garden keeps half its growth.
- Logged a slip by mistake? Undo it.

A GARDEN THAT GROWS WITH YOU (FREE)
- Every check-in waters a bonsai that grows from seed toward a full tree as your nicotine-free days add up.
- Completed trees stay in your garden.

APPLE WATCH AND WIDGETS (FREE)
- Your day count on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, in five widget styles.
- Apple Watch app with your day count, streak start date and garden stage.
- The count moves forward every day, even when you don't open the app.

NO ACCOUNT, PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No sign-up, email or login.
- Your quit date, check-ins, journal and cravings stay on your device.
- No ads and no cross-app tracking.

ALSO FREE
- The first five of 13 nicotine recovery milestones, with sources.
- Your first journal entry.

BLOOM+ (OPTIONAL)
- Your craving patterns: the hours and days urges hit, what sets them off, and how long yours last.
- The full nicotine recovery timeline with sources.
- Unlimited journaling with guided prompts.
- Achievements and milestone unlocks.
- Money, pouches and nicotine avoided since day one.
- More garden species to grow.
Eligible new subscribers may get a free trial.

Quit Zyn is built for stopping completely. Whether you're quitting Zyn, other nicotine pouches or snus, it counts your nicotine-free days, gives you something to do when a craving hits, and keeps your record when you slip.

Quit Zyn is a self-tracking tool, not medical advice. For help quitting, talk to a clinician or call 1-800-QUIT-NOW in the US.
