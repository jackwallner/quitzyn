Is there a Quit Zyn day counter for Apple Watch and the Lock Screen?

By Jack Wallner · Last updated October 30, 2026

Yes. Quit Zyn: Pouch & Snus Tracker has an Apple Watch app and three Lock Screen widgets that show your nicotine-free days, and all of them are free. It also has small and medium Home Screen widgets with your bonsai, and it needs no account.

Disclosure: Jack Wallner makes Quit Zyn, so this page describes his own app. Every detail about Quit Zyn below was checked against the app's current build.

General information, not medical advice. Quit Zyn is a self-tracking tool and does not treat nicotine dependence. For help quitting, talk to a clinician or call 1-800-QUIT-NOW (1-800-784-8669) in the US, and see Smokefree.gov.

Which widgets does Quit Zyn have?

SmallHome ScreenYour bonsai with the day count
MediumHome ScreenYour bonsai, day count, garden stage and start date

The widgets and the Watch app work the count out from your quit date, so it moves forward every day even when you have not opened the app.

What does the Apple Watch app show?

Open Quit Zyn on your Watch to see your day count, the day your current streak started and your garden's current stage. The Watch app is for seeing your count. Check-ins, cravings and slips are logged on your iPhone, and the Watch picks up the change.

How do you add the Lock Screen widget?

  1. Install Quit Zyn and set your quit date.
  2. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, then tap Customize and choose the Lock Screen.
  3. Tap the widget area under the clock (or the line above it for the inline widget) and pick Quit Zyn.
  4. On Apple Watch, Quit Zyn installs with the iPhone app when automatic install is on, or from Available Apps in the Watch app on your iPhone.

What the Watch app does not do yet

  • There is no watch face complication. For a number you can see all the time, use the Lock Screen widget on your iPhone.
  • You cannot check in from the Watch.

What about Android?

On Android, Quit Zyn has a Home Screen widget with the same day count and bonsai. It has no Lock Screen widgets and no watch app. See Quit Zyn for Android.

Frequently asked questions

Are Quit Zyn's widgets free?

Yes. All five widgets, three for the Lock Screen and two for the Home Screen, are in the free version.

Is the Quit Zyn Apple Watch app free?

Yes. It comes with the free iPhone app and shows your day count, the day your current streak started and your garden's stage.

Can I check in from my Apple Watch?

Not yet. The Watch app shows your count. Check-ins, cravings and slips are logged on your iPhone.

Does Quit Zyn have a watch face complication?

Not yet. The Lock Screen widgets on iPhone are the always-visible option today.

Do I need an account for the Watch to sync?

No. Your iPhone shares the count with your own Watch directly, with no account.
