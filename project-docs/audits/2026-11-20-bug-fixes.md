## Residual risks for a real device

- B1 runs before every launch. With an aside store present it opens the live and aside stores once each launch until one is put back. Moving SQLite files right after a container is released was only proven in the simulator.
- B1 under 20 MB free runs the session in memory: a user could onboard again there and lose it on the next launch (the real store is untouched).
- B7 relies on the wall clock after real suspension; only a main-thread stall was tested.
- B8 cold-launch notification tap was not observed on a device.
- B2's table is magnitudes, not exchange rates; a currency missing from it keeps the dollar scale. The Android port (`android/`) was not in scope and still has the dollar-sized steppers if it copied them.

## Needs Jack

1. B1: a user whose store was moved aside by an earlier build and who then onboarded again has history in both stores; the app keeps the new one. Recommend leaving it: merging is a feature, and the old store stays on disk.
2. B4: achievements unlocked during the mistaken date stay unlocked. Recommend leaving them: revoking a badge reads as a punishment for a typo.
