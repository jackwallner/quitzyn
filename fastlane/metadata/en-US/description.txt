Quit Zyn counts your days since you quit Zyn, snus or other nicotine pouches. It's free, needs no account, keeps your data on your device, and adds guided breathing for cravings, widgets and an Apple Watch app.

COUNT YOUR NICOTINE-FREE DAYS (FREE)
- Counts the days since your quit date, today or any date in the past.
- Shows your current streak, longest streak and total nicotine-free days on a calendar.
- Logs a one-tap daily check-in of how you feel.
- Reminds you to check in at the hour you choose.
- Also works for dip, vaping or cigarettes.

GUIDED BREATHING FOR CRAVINGS (FREE)
- Starts box breathing when you tap "I'm having a craving": in, hold, out, hold, four seconds each.
- Runs two minutes, with an extra minute when you need it.
- Logs how strong the craving was and its trigger.

LOGGING A SLIP (FREE)
- Restarts the day counter from a slip's date, today or earlier.
- Keeps your calendar, longest streak and total nicotine-free days after a slip.
- Keeps half your garden's growth after a slip.
- Undoes your latest slip if you logged it by mistake.

BONSAI GARDEN (FREE)
- Grows a bonsai from seed toward a full tree with each daily check-in.
- Keeps every tree you complete.

APPLE WATCH AND WIDGETS (FREE)
- Shows your day count in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.
- Shows your day count, streak start date and garden stage on Apple Watch.

NO ACCOUNT, ON-DEVICE DATA (FREE)
- Works with no sign-up, email or login.
- Stores your quit date, check-ins, journal and cravings on your device.
- Shows no ads and does no tracking across apps.

BLOOM+ (PRO)
- Shows your craving patterns: peak hours, weekdays, triggers and how long urges last.
- Unlocks all 13 nicotine recovery milestones, with sources.
- Adds unlimited journaling with guided prompts.
- Adds achievements and more garden species.
- Tracks money, pouches and nicotine avoided.

FREE / PRO
Free: everything marked FREE, plus the first 5 recovery milestones and one journal entry.
Pro (Bloom+): everything marked PRO. Monthly or yearly subscription with a free trial for eligible new subscribers, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions auto-renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Quit Zyn is a self-tracking tool and does not give medical advice. For help quitting, talk to a clinician or call 1-800-QUIT-NOW in the US.
